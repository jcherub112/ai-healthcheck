AI Workflow Health Check

Ready to diagnose your AI workflow?

This takes about 5 minutes. You'll answer 29 questions across five dimensions: time saved, error rate, adoption, satisfaction, and ROI.

At the end you'll get a scorecard with a score for each dimension and an honest read of where the workflow is strong or weak. The composite score is the weakest of the five — the workflow is only as healthy as its weakest gate.

Your answers stay on your device. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere in this version.
